## Data findings that shaped the contract (recorded honestly)

- Official train has 26 duplicated `Drug_ID` values (incl. 14 × `-`) and 3 duplicated raw SMILES
  pairs whose Y values DIFFER between rows. Therefore: row *position* is the authoritative line
  identity; `Drug_ID` uniqueness is enforced only where the blind-export contract needs it;
  duplicates keep every original row, no dedup/canonicalization/Y edits; duplicate raw-SMILES
  groups are forced wholly onto one side of the split (leakage fix vs v3's per-row shuffle).
- `seed 43` is used for BOTH split_config and head_config in the registered config. The split RNG
  is a dedicated `random.Random(43)` instance created and consumed strictly BEFORE any model
  construction/re-seeding, so split identity cannot depend on model init (assertion-free by
  construction; documented algorithm `CPython random.Random.shuffle`).
